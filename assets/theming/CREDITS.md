# Background photo credits

Used by [Lectio Theming](../../modules/Lectio-Theming.user.js) as the backdrop for its four background-pattern families. All photos are downloaded and re-hosted in this repo (rather than hot-linked) so they can't go stale and no third-party image host sees a viewer's IP on every Lectio page load. Each is used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free for commercial and noncommercial use).
